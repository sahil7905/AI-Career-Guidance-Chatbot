# Soft Skills, Resume and Placement Guidance
Employers such as HCL, TCS, Infosys, Wipro and Accenture hire freshers through aptitude tests, technical interviews and HR rounds. Prepare quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, one programming language, DBMS, operating systems, computer networks and your own projects. A strong resume is one page with a clear summary, skills, two or three projects with measurable outcomes, internships and certifications. Build a GitHub portfolio and a LinkedIn profile. Practise communication, teamwork, time management and adaptability. Mock interviews and explaining projects aloud improve confidence. Useful resources: PrepInsta, IndiaBix, GeeksforGeeks interview section, LeetCode easy problems, Pramp for mock interviews, NPTEL courses for certified learning. Continuous learning, internships and open-source contribution strongly improve placement chances.
